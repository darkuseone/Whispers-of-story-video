ОПИСАНИЕ
In 1590 John White came back to Roanoke on his granddaughter's third birthday and found one word cut into a post: CROATOAN. No bones. No cross of distress. For four hundred years we treated that carving like a curse. It was an address. This is a slow walk through White's own account, the hidden fort under a British Museum patch, the Dare Stones hoax, and the iron flakes on Hatteras — evidence-first, no jump scares, built to be left on in the dark.
