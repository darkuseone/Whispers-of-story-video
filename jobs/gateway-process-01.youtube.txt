## DESCRIPTION
In 1983, a senior U.S. Army Intelligence officer was ordered to investigate a secretive acoustic training protocol in the mountains of Virginia. His classified 29-page assessment did not describe weapons or satellites, but a rigorous synthesis of quantum mechanics, neurophysiology, and the holographic nature of reality.

What began as Cold War anxiety over Soviet psychotronic research resulted in one of the most astonishing philosophical and scientific dossiers in military history. From the acoustic chambers of the Monroe Institute to the holographic universe models of David Bohm and Karl Pribram, this is the calm, investigative history of the CIA's Gateway Process, the enigma of the missing Page 25, and what happens when modern physics confronts the boundaries of human perception.
