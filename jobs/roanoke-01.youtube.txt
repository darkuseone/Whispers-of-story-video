## DESCRIPTION
In 1590 John White came back to Roanoke on his granddaughter's third birthday and found one word cut into a post: CROATOAN. No bones. No cross of distress. For four hundred years we treated that carving like a curse. It was an address.

This is a slow walk through White's own account, the year the English made enemies on that coast, the hidden fort under a British Museum patch, the Dare Stones hoax, and the iron flakes on Hatteras. Evidence-first. No jump scares. Built to be left on in the dark.

Notes
Colonist counts vary by source (commonly 115–117); the video does not pretend there was a census. Site X and the 2025 Hatteras hammerscale are leads: English material on Algonquian sites in the right century, not a roll call of every 1587 name. The Dare Stones series is treated as a hoax; the first stone is left disputed. Jamestown-era rumors of English-clothed people inland are labeled as rumors. Virginia Dare is the first child born to English parents in North America, not the first European child in the Americas. This is not a ghost story and it does not speak for Croatoan / Hatteras descendant communities.
