ОПИСАНИЕ (копировать одним блоком)
In September 2026 a tracked drill went into the grassy, boat-shaped hill at Durupinar, south of Mount Ararat, and the headlines got there before the laboratory. The cores are in Ankara. The bit broke at about four or five meters on something the project hopes might be mineralized wood. Nobody has a result yet.

This is not a verdict on Noah's Ark. It is a slow walk through the instruments that have been aimed at the same oval since 1959: an aerial photograph, two days of digging, a metal detector, a geologist who signed a retraction, radar that is still preliminary, and a university permit that finally put steel in the ground. Evidence first. No jump scares. Built to be left on in the dark.

A general-interest historical investigation, not a religious ruling and not a claim that Noah's Ark has been found. The 1948 shepherd account is a local tradition. Dimensions of the formation circulate between about 157 and 164 meters; Fasold's tape of 538 feet is the long end of that range, and the match to 300 royal Egyptian cubits is only a length comparison. The 1960 expedition is reported as finding soil and rock; explosive use appears in some accounts and is treated as such. Radar corridors, right angles, three levels, the broken bit, and organic-chemistry multiples are project statements from 2024-2026, not peer-reviewed results. Pottery sherds have no published date. Laboratory news is expected in the winter of 2026-2027. The Ararat ice anomaly is a different site and is not investigated here.

ЗАКРЕПЛЁННЫЙ ПЕРВЫЙ КОММЕНТАРИЙ
The drill is real. The result is not in yet.
Cores from the Durupinar formation went to Ankara in September 2026. The university has not claimed Noah's Ark. The press release hopes the broken bit hit mineralized wood. Those are different sentences, and the episode keeps them apart.
If you want the piece that changed my mind while writing this, it is the 1996 Collins and Fasold paper: the oval as a doubly plunging syncline, the "brackets" as limonite. The new cores get to test that. They do not get to skip it.
