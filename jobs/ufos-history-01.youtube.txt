Notes
A note on sources: the Tulli Papyrus has never been physically located and is treated by most Egyptologists as unauthenticated (possibly a modern hoax); it is discussed here as a documented controversy, not a confirmed ancient record. Zecharia Sitchin's Anunnaki/Nibiru theory and the Vaimanika Shastra flight manual are both rejected by mainstream Assyriology and aeronautics respectively, and are presented as cultural phenomena, not endorsed claims. The "40% unresolved" figure in the final chapter is one scientist's public characterization of ongoing AARO casework in 2026, not an official government statistic — treat it as provisional.
